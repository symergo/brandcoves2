## The short version

We hold as little as the site can function on. You can search, discover and build
a list without giving us anything. If you give us an email address it is used to
send you the thing you asked for and nothing else, and you can delete everything
at any time.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.

## What we collect, and why

### If you never sign in

**A visitor identifier.** A cookie holding a random value, set the first time you
save something. It exists so that a wishlist works before you have an account.
Without it, "save this" would require a signup, which is how you lose the visit.
It is not linked to a name and it is not shared.

**Search terms.** What was typed, aggregated by the hour, with no link to you or
your identifier. We use it to decide which Coves to write. Because it is
aggregated on the way in, an individual search cannot be traced back to a person,
including by us.

**Basic technical data** in server logs: IP address, browser, the page requested.
Standard for operating a website, kept briefly, used for security and debugging.

### If you make an account

**Your email address**, because sign-in is by emailed link and there is nothing to
send without it. If you sign in with Google we also receive your name and profile
picture from them.

**Your lists**, and anything you write in them.

**Recipients.** If you build a list for someone else, we store whatever you enter
about them: a name, a note, sometimes a birthday. This is personal data about a
person who is not you and who has not agreed to anything, so please treat it
accordingly and keep it to what you need. It is deleted with your account.

**Alerts** you set, and the notifications produced by them.

### If you subscribe to the Daily Cove

**Your email address, the date you confirmed, and the IP address you signed up
from.** The IP exists to demonstrate consent if it is ever questioned, and to spot
a signup form being abused to mail someone who never asked. It is used for nothing
else.

Nothing is sent to an address that has not confirmed. Every email carries a
one-click unsubscribe, and it works permanently. The link in a message from three
years ago will still work.

## What we do not do

- **No advertising trackers.** No Meta pixel, no advertising cookies, no data
  broker.
- **No profile building.** We do not construct an interest profile from your
  browsing and we do not sell one.
- **No selling data.** Not to shops, not to networks, not to anyone.

Affiliate networks set their own tracking when you follow an outbound link, so
that a purchase can be attributed. That happens on their domain under their
policy, and is covered under "shops and networks" below.

## Where your data goes

**Shops and affiliate networks.** When you click through to a shop, that shop and
the network between us receive the fact that a visit came from Brandcoves,
normally as a tracking parameter. What they then do is governed by their own
privacy policies, not this one. We do not send them your email address, your
account or your lists.

**Our email provider**, to deliver sign-in links, alerts and the daily email.

**Our hosting provider**, which stores the database the site runs on. Servers are
in the European Union.

**A language model provider**, for the editorial text on Coves and Daily Coves.
Only product data is sent: titles, prices, categories. **No personal data is ever
included in a prompt**, and generation runs on a schedule rather than in response
to anything you do.

## How long we keep it

| | |
|---|---|
| Visitor cookie | 1 year from your last visit |
| Account, lists, recipients | Until you delete them |
| Search terms | 12 months, aggregated and unlinked from the start |
| Price history | 90 days |
| Newsletter subscription | Until you unsubscribe |
| Unconfirmed subscription | Deleted after 30 days |
| Server logs | 30 days |

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to show you what we hold, correct it, delete it,
hand it over in a portable format, or stop processing it. Where processing rests
on consent, as it does for the newsletter, you can withdraw that at any time, and
unsubscribing is exactly that.

Write to {{privacy_email}}. We will answer within 30 days, and we will not ask you
to justify the request.

Deleting your account removes your lists, your recipients, your alerts and your
notifications. It is immediate and it is not reversible.

If you think we have handled your data badly, you can complain to the Belgian Data
Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit / Autorité de protection des
données), Drukpersstraat 35, 1000 Brussels.

## Cookies

Four kinds, all of them necessary for something you asked for:

- **Session:** keeps you signed in.
- **Visitor identifier:** lets a wishlist work before you have an account.
- **Market:** remembers which country and language you chose.
- **CSRF token:** a security measure that stops another site submitting forms as
  you.

There are no analytics or advertising cookies, which is why there is no cookie
banner. Consent is required for cookies that are not strictly necessary, and we do
not set any.

## Children

Brandcoves is not aimed at children and we do not knowingly hold data about
anyone under 16. If you believe we do, write to {{privacy_email}} and it will be
deleted.

## Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, the date at the top changes and
anyone subscribed to the daily email is told in it. Older versions are available
on request.
